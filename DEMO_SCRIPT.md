## 🔬 PART 2: SIMULATOR WORKBENCH - Building Scenarios (4-5 minutes)

**[Click Start Simulation / Navigate to Simulator Workbench]**

**"Welcome to the Simulator Workbench - this is where the magic happens."**

### Creating a New Simulation
**[Click "New Simulation" button]**

**"Let's say you're planning your strategy for a specific account - maybe a customer called 'Acme Corporation.'**

**Creating a simulation is straightforward:**

1. **Enter the account name** - "Acme Corporation"
2. **Add the actions you're planning to take** - These could be things like:
   - Marketing campaigns
   - Product training sessions
   - Account manager check-ins
   - New feature deployments
   
**Let me show you how this works..."**

**[Create a sample simulation with actions]**

**"For each action, you enter:**
- **Action name** - What you're going to do (e.g., 'Quarterly Business Review')
- **Number of events** - How many times you'll do it (e.g., 4 times this year)
- **Current score** - Where the account stands today (e.g., 60 out of 100)
- **Proposed score** - Where you expect them to be after this action (e.g., 75 out of 100)

**The system automatically calculates:**
- **Complete Score** - The total impact when fully executed
- **Weight** - How important this action is relative to others
- **Account Score** - The overall predicted performance score
- **Major Contributor** - Which action drives the most improvement"**

### The Power of "What-If" Analysis
**"Here's where it gets really interesting. Watch what happens when I change the proposed score..."**

**[Edit a proposed score and show dynamic recalculation]**

**"See how everything updates instantly? This is real-time 'what-if' analysis.**

**You can ask questions like:**
- **'What if we double our training events?'**
- **'What if we achieve a higher engagement score?'**
- **'What's the minimum effort needed to move this account to the next tier?'**

**No spreadsheets, no complex formulas - just change the numbers and see the impact immediately."**

---

## 📤 PART 3: BULK UPLOAD - Scaling Your Simulations (2 minutes)

**[Navigate to Bulk Upload section]**

**"Now, you might be thinking: 'This is great for one account, but I have 50, 100, or 500 accounts.' That's where bulk upload comes in.**

**You can:**

1. **Download a simple Excel template** [Click "Download Template"]
2. **Fill it out with your account data** - your team can work on this offline
3. **Upload it all at once** [Show "Upload Accounts" button]

**The system processes hundreds of accounts in seconds, creating individual simulations for each one.**

**What's powerful here is that if you have multiple scenarios for the same account - say, a 'conservative approach' and an 'aggressive approach' - you can upload both. The system keeps them separate and lets you compare them side-by-side in the dropdown."**

### Editing Uploaded Data
**"And here's a key feature our clients love: even after uploading, the data is fully editable.**

**If market conditions change or you want to test a different assumption, just click on any number - Events, Proposed Scores - and the system recalculates everything automatically. No need to re-upload or start from scratch."**

---

## ✅ PART 4: COMMIT TO COHORTS - Making It Real (2 minutes)

**[Point to "Commit to Cohorts" button]**

**"Once you're confident in your simulation, you 'Commit to Cohorts.'**

**Think of this as saying, 'Okay, I believe in this scenario. Let's track it as our baseline expectation.'**

**When you commit:**
- **Accounts are classified into performance cohorts** (Cohort 1: Top Performers, Cohort 2: Average, Cohort 3: Needs Attention)
- **You can now track them over time**
- **You can run year-over-year comparisons**

**This is how you turn simulations into actionable strategies with measurable KPIs."**

## 📈 PART 5: COHORT ANALYSIS - Measuring Success (3-4 minutes)

**[Navigate to Cohort Analysis Tab]**

**"After committing your simulations, the Cohort Analysis tab becomes your performance tracking center."**

### Year Selection & Cohort Flow
**"First, you can select which fiscal year you want to analyze."**

**[Show year selection buttons]**

**"The Cohort Flow Visualization shows you account movement between cohorts:**
- **Green numbers: accounts moving UP (gains)**
- **Red numbers: accounts moving DOWN (losses)**
- **This tells you: 'Are our strategies working? Are accounts improving or declining?'"**

### Cohort Metrics Table
**[Scroll to Cohort Metrics table]**

**"This table breaks down the performance metrics for each cohort:**
- **Number of accounts in each tier**
- **Total revenue contribution**
- **Average account score**
- **Year-over-year changes**

**Notice how the metrics are organized as rows and cohorts as columns? This makes it easy to compare performance across cohorts at a glance.**

**For example, you might see:**
- **Cohort 1 (Top Performers): 25 accounts generating $5M in revenue**
- **Cohort 2 (Average): 50 accounts generating $3M in revenue**
- **Cohort 3 (Needs Attention): 30 accounts generating $1M in revenue**

**This helps you identify where to focus: Do you invest in moving Cohort 2 accounts to Cohort 1? Or do you fix the issues in Cohort 3?"**

### Year-over-Year Delta Analysis
**[Scroll to Delta Analysis table]**

**"The Delta Analysis table shows you the changes between years:**
- **How many accounts moved up or down?**
- **How did revenue change?**
- **What's the overall trend?**

**This is your proof point. When your CFO or CEO asks, 'Did our strategy work?' - this is where you show the evidence.**

**For instance:**
- **'We invested in 20 Cohort 2 accounts, and 15 of them moved to Cohort 1, increasing revenue by $2M'**
- **Or: 'We identified 10 at-risk Cohort 3 accounts early and prevented $500K in churn'"**

## 📝 DEMO TIPS & BEST PRACTICES

### Before the Demo:
1. ✅ **Prepare realistic sample data** - Use account names and scenarios that mirror the customer's industry
2. ✅ **Test all features** - Run through the demo once to avoid technical issues
3. ✅ **Have backup scenarios ready** - In case the customer asks to see specific examples
4. ✅ **Know your customer's pain points** - Tailor the emphasis (e.g., more on ROI tracking vs. more on bulk processing)

### During the Demo:
1. ✅ **Pause for questions** - Don't rush; engagement is more valuable than coverage
2. ✅ **Use business language** - Avoid terms like "data models," "algorithms," "UI/UX"
3. ✅ **Let them drive** - If they want to see something specific, adapt
4. ✅ **Tell stories, not features** - "This helped Company X identify $2M in at-risk revenue" vs. "This is the cohort analysis feature"

### After the Demo:
1. ✅ **Send a follow-up summary** - Key takeaways and next steps
2. ✅ **Share a recorded demo** - So they can review with stakeholders
3. ✅ **Provide a trial/sandbox** - Let them explore hands-on
4. ✅ **Schedule a follow-up** - Strike while the iron is hot
